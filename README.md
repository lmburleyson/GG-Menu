# The Green Gyft Menu Board

A simple web page for updating the TV menus at the three Green Gyft stores and exporting them as PNG images.

**Live site:** https://lmburleyson.github.io/GG-Menu/

**Stores:** Charlotte (CLT), Salem, Mooresville

Everything lives in one file, `index.html`. There is nothing to install and no server.

## How to use it

1. Open https://lmburleyson.github.io/GG-Menu/ in **Chrome or Edge** on a computer.
2. Pick your store at the top.
3. Use the tabs:
   - **Stock**: tap a strain to switch it between *in stock* and *sold out*.
   - **Edit menu**: change names, descriptions, prices and discounts.
   - **Preview**: see exactly what the TV image will look like.
4. Click **Export**.
   - CLT exports one image.
   - Salem (4 TVs) and Mooresville (3 TVs) export one image per TV. Choose a folder when asked, or unzip the `.zip` you get.
   - Use **Export this TV** to export only the TV you're looking at.
5. Put the PNGs on the TVs the same way you do today.

Images are 1920 x 1080 (TV size).

## Good to know

- Each store keeps its own stock. Changing Salem never changes Mooresville or CLT.
- Edits save automatically **in your browser on that computer**. They are not shared between computers.
- To move a store's menu to another computer, click **Save data**, then **Load data** on the other computer.
- **Reset** in the Edit tab puts that store back to the original TV menus.
- Clearing your browser's site data erases saved edits. Use **Save data** first if you want a backup.



## Notes

- Photos are built into the file, so it works without internet once loaded.
- PNG export is tested in Chrome and Edge. Safari and phones may not work.
- The page asks search engines not to list it, but anyone with the link can open it. Don't put anything private in it.
